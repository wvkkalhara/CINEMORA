Meet the Founder

W. Thusal Prabhasha Methshan De Silva

Founder & Creative Director of Cinemora Production

20 years oldBalapitiya, Sri LankaUniversity of Westminster Foundation

I am W. Thusal Prabhasha Methshan De Silva, a 20-year-old entrepreneur and creative filmmaker from Balapitiya, Sri Lanka.

I successfully completed my G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L) examinations and further completed the Foundation Level programme at the University of Westminster, following the Computing Science pathway.

My passion for creativity, photography, and videography inspired me to establish Cinemora Production, a creative production brand built around capturing emotions, telling stories, and transforming special moments into timeless visual experiences.

As the founder, I focus on developing Cinemora into more than just a photography and videography business. My vision is to build a professional creative production company that delivers high-quality visual content, innovative concepts, and exceptional experiences for every client.

Through Cinemora Production, I aim to explore new creative possibilities, work with different individuals and brands, cover memorable events, and continuously improve my skills in filmmaking, cinematography, photography, and visual storytelling.

I strongly believe that success is not achieved overnight. It is built through dedication, consistency, creativity, and the courage to keep moving forward.

Cinemora is not just a business I created; it is a vision I am committed to building. And this is only the beginning.
